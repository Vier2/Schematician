## What is Schematician?

## What unique value does it have?

1. Entities: Typically, note taking or documentation applications store information in arbitrary forms like "notes", "pages", "vaults", 
"folders". Our brain don't store information like this. We synthesize information into entities, things, objects. Our understanding of these things develop overtime, and these things are persistent.
 So Schematician organizes information in this way too. Information is stored in things called "Schemas" or "Instances". These are persistent objects which can accumulate any amount of information and be referenced as a dynamic source. 
 What problem does this solve?
    1.1 Typically students receive information about concepts they want to learn, and their notes are recreated when turning new information, even about concepts previously documented, because these concepts aren't referenable on their own. Schematician allows someone to define a entity of information, and continue adding data to that entity, and reference this anywhere else in the app. So it's memory is not lost or scattered.
